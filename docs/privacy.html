Power View for Jira — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Power View for Jira uses your existing browser session to display and update Jira data. Jira content is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to the extension author or stored on an external server.

The extension may access Jira pages and data only to provide its planning, filtering, visualization, and explicitly confirmed editing features. The extension does not sell, transfer, or use data for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

Settings and cached view preferences may be stored locally in Chrome storage. You can remove them by uninstalling the extension or clearing its site data.

For questions, contact the publisher through the Chrome Web Store listing.
